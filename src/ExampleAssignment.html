Dynamic Programming Homework (20 points)

For this assignment, you will design and implement an algorithm that calculates the probability of success for the board game Risk. The official instructions for the game can be found at https://instructions.hasbro.com/en-us/instruction/Risk-Game. The relevant part of the instructions for this assignment come from page 4 of  the instructions. In short, the attacker and defender each have a number of units. The attacker gets three six-sided dice and the defender gets two, unless they have fewer units than that. They each roll and the highest results are paired against each other. For each result, the lower paired die’s owner loses a unit with the defender winning ties. Here are some examples from the official rules:
